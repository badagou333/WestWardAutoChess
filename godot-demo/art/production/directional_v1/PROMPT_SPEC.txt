Reconstructed production specification for the first seven character atlases, not a verbatim tool-call transcript.
Tool: built-in image_gen; transparent_background: true. No CLI/API fallback.
Use case: identity-preserve / stylized-concept game sprite atlas.
Input: the approved corresponding character concept under art/concepts/<role>/nine_sols_v1/ (hunter uses nine_sols_archer_v1). Preserve existing costume palette, equipment, species and silhouette.
Output: one transparent square sheet, four rows and four columns, sixteen full-body isolated key poses, uniform character scale. Fixed orthographic elevated view around45 degrees; visible head/shoulder tops, no eye-level portrait. First two rows three-quarter front facing lower-right; last two rows genuine rear facing upper-right with no face turned toward viewer. Full weapon/tail/hat in frame, generous clear gaps, no floor/shadow/background/labels/grid.
Pose order within each bank of eight: idle, movement contact, movement passing/opposite support, attack windup, attack release/impact, hit recoil, collapse beginning, collapsed final. Keep weapons attached, correct number of limbs, distinct action silhouettes. Effects are separate except fox's small hand-held flames.
Identity anchors: Tang Sanzang gold/red robe and crown with one staff; monk badger yellow robe/white collar and one long staff; hunter red panda green cloak/straw hat/bow and quiver; sparrow blue/tan plumage and head feather; herbal child grey/sage robes, herb hat and ring staff; fox white/orange fur, charcoal robe/red scarf/two separate hand flames/rear tail; mountain wolf quadruped grey fur, no equipment.
Corrections recorded separately: Tang duplicated staff removal, sparrow feather-tip padding, monk/hunter/fox movement-passing replacements. Single-rectangle overlap is handled by engine UV geometry, not by modifying PNG pixels. Original two similar movement drawings were not accepted as a complete alternating walk cycle.
