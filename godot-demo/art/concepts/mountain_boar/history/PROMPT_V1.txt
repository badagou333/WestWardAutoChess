Use case: stylized-concept
Asset: one original ordinary wild mountain boar enemy design candidate for a Chinese folklore 2D tactical board game. It is a fast breakthrough animal, NOT a boss, armored elite or humanoid.
Subject: full-body quadruped wild boar in three-quarter side view facing LEFT, alert idle stance with four separate grounded cloven hooves. Compact low wedge-shaped body, powerful but not oversized shoulder, sloping back, short sturdy athletic legs, elongated coarse snout with a broad dark nose, small triangular ears, two modest ivory tusks, short thin tail with bristly tip. Make head, snout, tusks and hooves readable at about 100px tall; distinguish its stocky low silhouette from a long-legged wolf. Keep leg joints plausible for a boar.
Style: dark earthy hand-painted Chinese folklore game illustration, strong irregular dark ink outline, broad muted color planes with controlled dry-brush bristle texture, slightly exaggerated animation-friendly shapes, not photoreal and not a cute domestic pig. Dark umber and warm gray-brown coat, a restrained charcoal bristle ridge on shoulders, lighter dusty snout and lower legs, small non-glowing amber eyes. Simpler texture than a hero; ordinary forest animal with no costume or accessories.
Composition: exactly one centered complete figure with generous transparent margin around snout, tusks, ears, tail and all hooves. Genuine transparent PNG alpha background. No shadows, ground, landscape, aura, haze or checkerboard; no text, grid or other poses.
Avoid: human stance, clothing, armor, weapons, runes, oversized demon horns/tusks, giant boss proportions, magic, blood, wounds, companions. This is a first design proposal only, not an action sheet.
