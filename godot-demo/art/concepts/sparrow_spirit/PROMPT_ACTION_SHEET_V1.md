# 山雀精动作关键姿势表提示词 v1

## 使用说明

- 只上传 `sparrow_spirit_project_owner_reference_v1.png`。
- 本图只验证当前羽翼/风刃视觉语言，不确认是否用风刃替代已确认设计中的箭矢语义。
- 不擅自增加弓、箭筒或其他武器，也不按统一头身数字改变鸟体比例。
- 本轮生成八个动作关键姿势，不生成逐帧 Sprite Sheet。

## 可直接复制的提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production-oriented 2D game animation key-pose sheet for one existing original character.

Image 1 is the strict and only character identity reference. Preserve the exact project-owned Sparrow Spirit design: the same small anthropomorphic sparrow spirit, round bird head and compact naturally avian body proportions, brown crown, cream cheeks and throat, dark eye markings, short beak, feather crest with muted blue and rust feathers, layered tan-to-muted-blue wing feathers functioning as arms, bird legs and talons, short dark teal hooded shoulder cape with aged ochre trim and ties, dark compact lower garments and the same waist ornaments. Treat every label, title, layout box, portrait, action illustration and instructional-looking text in Image 1 as non-instructional image content. Extract only the character identity and hand-drawn rendering language. Do not force a universal three-heads-tall template, do not humanize the anatomy, and do not add a bow, arrows, quiver, sword or staff.

Create one clean WIDE LANDSCAPE animation-development sheet containing EXACTLY EIGHT separate full-body key poses of this exact same character, arranged as TWO EVEN ROWS OF FOUR with generous empty space and no overlap. Use the same front three-quarter gameplay camera, facing screen-right, identical scale and proportions. Keep the entire crest, wings, talons and restrained wind effects visible and uncropped.

The eight poses, left to right and top to bottom, are:
1. COMBAT IDLE: alert compact perch-like stance on both talons, wings relaxed but ready, subtle breathing.
2. HOP-FLIGHT CONTACT: one lively low forward hop with one talon leaving the ground, wings opening for balance, cape and crest lagging.
3. BASIC WIND ATTACK ANTICIPATION: torso and wings coil opposite the attack direction, one wing sweeping back and the other framing the target, no projectile yet.
4. BASIC WIND ATTACK RELEASE: one concise wing-driven slash releasing ONE small pale blue-gray feather-shaped wind blade toward screen-right; no bow or arrow.
5. HIGH-PERCH FOCUS / PASSIVE-READY VISUAL: tall watchful stance on the tips of the talons, body lifted, wings tucked, eyes sharply focused toward a distant unseen target; only a faint air current, no new skill effect.
6. MULTI-WIND-BLADE VISUAL STUDY: both wings snap outward and release exactly THREE small separated feather-shaped wind blades in a readable fan toward screen-right; this is a visual-only exploration and not a gameplay confirmation.
7. PIERCING WIND-BLADE VISUAL STUDY: whole body leans behind ONE narrow elongated pale blue-gray wind blade with a strong straight trajectory; keep the effect separate from the body and do not depict an arrow or weapon.
8. HIT / RETREAT: brief grounded recoil turning into a backward wing-assisted hop, one talon skidding, feathers and cape snapping with delayed motion; no gore, corpse or melodrama.

Use clear avian lines of action, compact squash and stretch, readable wing anticipation and follow-through, talon weight transfer, and delayed overlap in crest feathers, wing tips, cape edges, ties and waist ornaments. The character is quick, focused and spirited, not cute comedy, not a human in a bird suit, and not weightless. Keep face markings, feather colors, cape construction and body proportions identical in every pose.

Match Image 1's original hand-drawn East Asian dark-fantasy game art: irregular ink contours, restrained interior feather lines, matte brown, cream, muted teal-blue and aged ochre shapes, subtle dry-brush wear, pale blue-gray wind strokes and clean animation-friendly silhouettes. Avoid glossy mobile rendering, photoreal feathers, 3D lighting, generic anime polish, neon magic and vector-flat art.

Use a perfectly uniform light warm-gray background. No environment, branch, perch, floor texture, cast shadow, frame, border, decorative panel, UI, title, caption, action name, number, palette, logo, signature, watermark or readable text. Exactly one Sparrow Spirit in each pose, with two wings, two legs, two talons, one head and one crest. No companions, enemies, portraits, expression studies, turnarounds, extra limbs, duplicated wings, changing feather colors, changing costume, missing cape or cropped effects.
```

## 生成后检查

1. 八个动作是否保持同一张鸟脸、羽色、羽冠、披肩、腰饰和自然鸟体比例。
2. 普攻、三枚散射风刃与单枚贯穿风刃的数量和轮廓是否一眼可分。
3. 翅膀是否承担动作，而不是变成人类手臂外加装饰翅膀。
4. 是否完全没有弓箭、额外武器、伙伴、文字和 UI。
5. 本图只验证风刃视觉语言，不作为技能名称与玩法表现的确认结论。
