# 护寺棍僧方案 3 单角色生成提示词

## 当前状态

- 项目负责人认为六方案中的第 3 个最符合心中的护寺棍僧。
- 这是下一张单角色探索图的优先方向，不代表最终造型已经确认。
- 《九日》图片仅用于画风分析，不复制任何具体角色设计。

## 输入图片角色

- 图片 1～N：《九日》角色或战斗画面，只作为线条、色块、动态构图、材质克制程度和暗色氛围参考。
- 最后一张项目图片：`guard_monk_shape_exploration_v2.png`，只参考从左到右第 3 个方案的三头身比例、三角形轮廓、低姿态和斜持木棍方式。
- 不将任何输入图片作为直接编辑目标。

## 可直接复制的提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original full-body character exploration for a 2D tactical auto-battler game.

Input images:
- Images 1 through N are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY. Analyze their bold hand-drawn contour language, expressive line-weight changes, large graphic color masses, restrained internal detail, strong asymmetrical posing, dramatic negative space, matte painted finish, and dark East Asian fantasy atmosphere. Do not copy, remix, or reproduce any character, costume, anatomy, weapon, pose, symbol, calligraphy, composition, or story element from them.
- The final project image is a SHAPE REFERENCE ONLY. Use only alternative number 3, the top-right monk: preserve its compact roughly three-head-tall adult proportion, sharp triangular silhouette, low braced center of gravity, wide grounded stance, and long diagonal staff gesture. Do not copy the other five alternatives.

Primary request: Design one completely original first-star hero named “Temple Staff Guardian Monk” (护寺棍僧). He is an adult human Chinese temple guard who protects one selected ally and controls nearby enemies by striking the ground with his staff. His appearance must communicate steady defense, alertness, discipline, and contained strength. He is a dependable guardian, not a raging warrior, healer, priest, or bodybuilder.

Character design:
- shaved head; compact adult face with a stern but calm expression;
- exactly about three heads tall, with a large readable head, compact torso, short powerful legs, and slightly oversized hands and feet for small-screen readability;
- lean triangular body silhouette rather than a round or bulky body;
- low defensive three-quarter stance, front foot planted, rear leg braced;
- a plain long hardwood staff with small dark iron end caps, held on a strong diagonal across the composition;
- one open hand subtly guarding an unseen teammate while the other controls the staff;
- practical layered temple work clothes: a short asymmetrical charcoal outer robe, muted deep-teal cloth panels, weathered ochre inner layer, loose cropped trousers, cloth wraps, simple shoes, and one restrained aged-red waist accent;
- humble first-star equipment with visible wear, no luxurious ornament and no elaborate embroidery;
- use garment planes and stance to express protection; do not add a visible magical shield.

Style and rendering: original dark 2D hand-drawn East Asian fantasy animation art. Bold slightly rough ink-like outer contours, sparse expressive interior lines, 3–5 large flat color masses, one restrained cel-shadow plane, subtle dry-brush texture, matte mineral pigments, and selective high-saturation accents. Sophisticated, graphic, energetic, and animation-ready. Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering and realistic concept-art rendering.

Composition: exactly one full-body character, completely visible from head to feet with the whole staff uncropped, centered on a plain desaturated warm-gray background. Dynamic three-quarter defensive pose with a clear silhouette and generous negative space. No environment, no UI, no model-sheet panels, no turnaround views, no accessory callouts, and no text.

Color palette: charcoal black, deep muted teal, weathered ochre, warm gray cloth, dark hardwood brown, and a very small aged-red accent. Keep the palette limited and readable against dark battlefields.

Constraints: create an original design belonging only to this project. Do not imitate or resemble any specific existing game character. No animal head, fox or cat features, mask, futuristic device, glowing weapon, energy blade, magic aura, halo, armor, cape, giant prayer beads, ringed monk staff, ornate religious symbols, calligraphy, logo, watermark, extra character, duplicated limb, childlike body, realistic muscular anatomy, glossy 3D, generic anime rendering, or detailed background.
```

如果图像工具只支持一张参考图，优先使用项目自己的六方案图，并把《九日》的视觉特征保留为文字描述；不要只上传某一个《九日》角色并要求直接改成和尚。
