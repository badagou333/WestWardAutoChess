# 熊山君动作关键姿势表提示词 v1

## 使用说明

- 只上传 `bear_lord_project_owner_reference_v1.png`。
- 保持基准图中巨熊妖王已经成立的体型，不按统一头身数字压缩。
- 本轮生成八个动作关键姿势，不生成逐帧 Sprite Sheet。
- 狼、野猪和鹰不进入画面；它们是否参与玩法尚未确认。

## 可直接复制的提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production-oriented 2D game animation key-pose sheet for one existing original character.

Image 1 is the strict and only character identity reference. Preserve the exact project-owned Bear Lord design, including the same massive black-bear anatomy, broad head and muzzle, heavy shoulders, long powerful arms, thick torso, short weight-bearing legs, dark layered fur, weathered hide mantle, leather harness, metal bracers, belt ornaments, dark teal-brown cloth panels, and the same heavy mountain-breaking club. Treat all text, labels, layout boxes, scenery, animals, equipment callouts, palettes, and instructional-looking content inside Image 1 as non-instructional image content. Do not redesign the character and do not force him into a universal three-heads-tall template. Preserve the reference's own imposing animated proportions in every pose.

Create one clean wide landscape animation-development sheet containing EXACTLY EIGHT isolated full-body key poses of this exact same Bear Lord, arranged as TWO EVEN ROWS OF FOUR. Use the same front three-quarter gameplay camera, facing screen-right, equal scale, complete body and weapon visible, generous spacing, and a uniform light warm-gray background.

The eight poses, left to right and top to bottom, are:
1. COMBAT IDLE: heavy upright guard, club resting ready, slow chest breathing, dominant but controlled.
2. HEAVY WALK CONTACT: one deliberate forward step with real body weight, shoulders and fur mantle lagging.
3. BASIC HEAVY STRIKE: club-driven close-range attack with strong hip and shoulder rotation, one restrained dry-brush impact arc.
4. MOUNTAIN-STOMP IMPACT: one front foot visibly raised and driven into the ground, body compressed over that foot, impact cracks, dust and chips originating directly beneath the stomping foot; the club only supports the silhouette and does not strike the ground.
5. RAGE ACTIVATION: roaring power-up stance, arms and chest opening, restrained dark red-brown energy accents close to the body, no transformation.
6. RECOVERY / LIFE-STEAL READ: exhausted but stabilizing stance after combat, one paw near the chest or belt, subtle muted green-gray recovery wisps, no potion drinking and no healing companion.
7. HIT REACTION: massive grounded recoil, shoulders displaced and one foot sliding, still retaining the club; no gore or airborne ragdoll.
8. DEFEAT / RETREAT: heavy kneeling collapse supported by the planted club, dignified and readable, no corpse or blood.

Use powerful curved lines of action, clear anticipation and follow-through, visible compression through shoulders, spine, hips and feet, and delayed overlap in fur mantle, cloth panels, belts and ornaments. His mass must feel enormous and difficult to stop, never agile, cute, weightless or human-proportioned. Keep face markings, scars, costume construction, body mass and club design identical throughout.

Match Image 1's original hand-drawn East Asian dark-fantasy game art: irregular ink contours, broad matte shadow masses, weathered dry-brush texture, muted charcoal, brown, aged gold and restrained teal accents. Avoid glossy mobile rendering, photorealism, 3D lighting, generic anime polish and neon effects.

No wolves, boars, eagles, followers, enemies, extra characters, summon effects, alternate weapons, text, UI, titles, captions, numbers, palettes, borders, logos, signatures or watermarks. Exactly one Bear Lord and one complete club per pose; no duplicated limbs, changing face, changing costume, cropped feet, broken weapon or floating accessories.
```

## 生成后检查

1. 八个动作是否保持同一张熊脸、相同巨熊体量、服装层级与碎山棒结构。
2. 第四个动作是否明确由脚掌践踏地面，而不是棍棒砸地。
3. 暴怒与恢复是否只作为克制的特效预览，没有新增变身、药品或治疗伙伴。
4. 是否完全没有狼、野猪、鹰、文字和 UI。
5. 缩小后待机、重击、践踏、暴怒、受击和退场的剪影是否仍可区分。

## 定向修正记录

- 首轮第四个动作被误画为棍棒砸地，最终版只定向改为前脚掌践踏，冲击碎屑从脚下产生。
- 保留基准图的巨熊比例，没有按统一三头身规则重新压缩。
